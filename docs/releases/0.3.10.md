**中文**

- **修复** 清理遇到 Core Audio `!dev` / `!obj` 时，核实对象已从 HAL 枚举消失后才继续释放；仍存在或无法确认的资源保留句柄，不提前释放回调内存。
- **修复** 退出不再静默取消：正常释放最多等待三秒；真实失败或超时后，可取消或选择仍然退出。仍然退出可能恢复应用原声，残留多输出设备可能需要手动移除；不会继续执行更新或重启。
- **重构** 应用音频的启动、切换和释放共用资源生命周期，清理重试只执行尚未完成的步骤。停止请求先关闭重放开关，不改写用户保存的音量或静音。
- **修复** 应用静音捕获更新失败时保留操作名和 Core Audio 状态码，不再出现只有应用名和冒号的红字。
- **改进** 多设备组少一台时继续使用原先选中的在线目标；意外接替到组外输出时只静音该设备一次，用户手动取消静音后不再重复写入。
- **修复** 音频进程只读观察恢复后，同一会话可以清除自身的临时读取错误；真实的处理、暂停和清理错误不会被自动清除。

**English**

- **Fixed** Cleanup now verifies HAL object disappearance after `!dev` / `!obj` instead of relying on the error code alone. Resources that still exist, or cannot be checked, retain their handles and callback memory.
- **Fixed** Quit no longer silently cancels. Normal cleanup waits up to three seconds; failure or timeout offers Cancel and Quit Anyway. Quitting anyway may restore original app audio or leave a multi-output device to remove manually, and cancels pending updates and restarts.
- **Refactored** App audio startup, output switching, and release share one resource lifecycle. Cleanup retries resume at the unfinished step. Stopping closes replay without changing saved gain or mute settings.
- **Fixed** App mute-tap update failures retain the operation name and Core Audio status instead of showing only an app name and colon.
- **Improved** Multi-output routes keep playing through the remaining selected online targets. An unexpected out-of-group successor is muted once, and a manual unmute is respected.
- **Fixed** A recovered read-only audio-process observation clears only its own temporary error. Real processing, suspension, and cleanup failures stay latched.
