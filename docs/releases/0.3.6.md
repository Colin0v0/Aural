**中文**

- **修复** 切换音箱、耳机或多设备输出成员时，已静音或压低音量的应用会短暂恢复原声的问题。
- **修复** 休眠唤醒、设备状态短暂不可信、进程读取失败和音频路由重试时，应用静音可能失效的问题。
- **修复** HAL 长期保留已销毁设备对象时，输出区域持续显示“读取音频名称失败（Core Audio 2003332927）”的问题。
- **改进** 输出切换失败后复用原有静音音频捕获，重新应用时不再重建整个捕获链。

**English**

- **Fixed** Apps that were muted or turned down could briefly leak original audio while switching speakers, headphones, or multi-output members.
- **Fixed** App mute could be lost during sleep/wake, transient device-state failures, process enumeration errors, and audio-route retries.
- **Fixed** A stale HAL device object could leave the output area showing “Failed to read audio name (Core Audio 2003332927)” indefinitely.
- **Improved** Failed output switches keep the existing mute capture alive, and retries reuse it instead of rebuilding the whole capture chain.
